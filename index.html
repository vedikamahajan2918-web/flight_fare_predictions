How it works

The estimate comes from a Random Forest model trained on hundreds of thousands of real domestic fares.

1

Enter your trip

Choose the route, airline, class, timings and how far ahead you are booking.

2

The model compares

It matches your trip against fare patterns for similar flights it has already seen.

3

You see the estimate

Get the expected fare, the cheapest booking window and a side-by-side airline comparison.
